Research

I work on self-supervised learning for Earth observation using paired Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery. A large part of my work is on the data side: how to build open pretraining corpora whose geographic and land-cover coverage is controlled by design rather than inherited from whatever imagery is easiest to download.

Before the PhD I worked as a software engineer, and I try to bring the same habits to research: pinned versions, checksums for every artifact, and pipelines that fail loudly instead of producing quiet nonsense.
